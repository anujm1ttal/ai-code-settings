# Visual Domain Presets

> Use these presets to define the "Style/Format" and constraints based on the project type.

## 1. Domain Mode: `youtube` (Thumbnails)
- **Aspect Ratio**: 16:9 (`--ar 16:9`)
- **Core Principle**: High click-through rate. Must be readable at 1 inch wide on a phone screen.
- **Modifiers to Include**: 
  - `High contrast, vibrant colors, clear focal point, hyper-detailed subject, blurred background (depth of field), dramatic cinematic lighting, YouTube thumbnail style.`
- **Content Rules**: Maximum 2 subjects. Faces must have exaggerated, strong expressions. Keep the right side of the image clear of critical details (where the timestamp goes).

## 2. Domain Mode: `pptx` (Presentation Graphics)
- **Aspect Ratio**: 16:9 for full bleed backgrounds, 1:1 for insert graphics.
- **Core Principle**: Clean, professional, and uncluttered. It must not distract from the presenter or the text on the slide.
- **Modifiers to Include**:
  - `Flat vector illustration, minimalist corporate style, clean white background, isometric data visualization, uncluttered, professional, high-key lighting.`
- **Content Rules**: "Earned Space" — leave plenty of negative space (empty areas) on either the left or right side of the image so that text can be overlaid cleanly in PowerPoint. Avoid busy or complex backgrounds.

## 3. Domain Mode: `concept-art` (General Vision)
- **Aspect Ratio**: Varies (usually 16:9 or 21:9)
- **Core Principle**: Mood and atmosphere over explicit detail.
- **Modifiers**: `Concept art, trending on ArtStation, epic scale, atmospheric haze, digital painting, majestic.`
